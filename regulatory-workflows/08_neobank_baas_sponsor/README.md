# Neobank BaaS Sponsor Bank Oversight

## Regulatory Overview

This example demonstrates Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS) sponsor bank oversight and compliance management for neobank partnerships. Sponsor banks must maintain comprehensive oversight of their fintech partners while ensuring that all banking activities conducted under their charter comply with applicable regulations, regardless of the third-party technology provider or customer-facing brand.

**Primary Regulations:**
- OCC Supervisory Guidance on Third-Party Relationships - SR 11-7
- FDIC Third-Party Risk Management Guidelines - FIL-44-2008
- Federal Reserve Commercial Bank Examination Manual (Outsourcing Technology)
- Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) Third-Party Oversight
- Community Reinvestment Act (CRA) assessment area considerations

**Key Compliance Requirements:**
- Comprehensive third-party risk management program with ongoing monitoring
- Technology service provider oversight with cybersecurity and operational resilience
- Customer complaint resolution with sponsor bank ultimate responsibility
- Regulatory examination cooperation and documentation accessibility
- Brand management and customer disclosure of sponsor bank relationship
- Capital adequacy and liquidity management for partner-generated deposits

## Business Context

A community bank sponsors multiple fintech partnerships providing digital banking services, payment processing, and lending products under the bank's charter. The bank must maintain effective oversight of each partnership while ensuring compliance with all applicable banking regulations, even when customer-facing operations are entirely managed by fintech partners.

**Common Examination Focus Areas:**
- OCC technology risk management examinations with fintech oversight assessment
- FDIC deposit insurance compliance with customer disclosure requirements
- CFPB consumer protection examination of partner-provided services
- CRA compliance with assessment area definition and community benefit evaluation
- Capital and liquidity management for rapid deposit growth scenarios

## Technical Implementation

The example simulates comprehensive BaaS oversight with multi-partner compliance monitoring:

1. **Partner Onboarding Management**: Due diligence and risk assessment for new fintech partnerships
2. **Real-Time Transaction Oversight**: AI-powered monitoring of partner-generated banking activity
3. **Compliance Dashboard Integration**: Centralized monitoring across multiple partner relationships
4. **Customer Experience Management**: End-to-end oversight of partner-provided customer services
5. **Regulatory Reporting Consolidation**: Unified regulatory reporting across all partnership activities
6. **Risk Management Integration**: Consolidated risk assessment and mitigation across partner portfolio
