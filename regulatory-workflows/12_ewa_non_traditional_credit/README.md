# Earned Wage Access & Non-Traditional Credit (CFPB/TILA)

## Regulatory Overview

This example demonstrates Earned Wage Access (EWA) platform compliance with evolving Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) guidance and Truth in Lending Act (TILA) disclosure requirements. EWA products occupy a complex regulatory landscape where classification uncertainty requires adaptive compliance systems that can adjust to changing guidance while maintaining comprehensive audit trails for examination readiness.

**Primary Regulations:**
- Truth in Lending Act (TILA) - 15 USC 1601-1667f
- Consumer Financial Protection Bureau Act - 12 USC 5511-5641
- Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) - 15 USC 1681-1681x
- Electronic Fund Transfer Act (EFTA) - 15 USC 1693-1693r
- Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA) - 15 USC 1692-1692p (if applicable)

**Key Compliance Requirements:**
- Regulatory classification tracking with evolving guidance adaptation
- TILA disclosure requirements based on current product classification status
- Ability-to-repay assessment and documentation for consumer protection
- Payroll integration verification with employer consent and wage validation
- Fee structure transparency with APR calculation and disclosure requirements
- Consumer protection compliance regardless of regulatory classification uncertainty

## Business Context

An EWA platform provider offers employees access to earned wages before scheduled paydays through employer partnerships and direct integrations. As regulatory guidance evolves, the platform must maintain compliance flexibility while preserving detailed audit trails that demonstrate consumer protection regardless of whether EWA products are ultimately classified as payroll advances, employer benefits, or credit products.

**Common Examination Focus Areas:**
- CFPB consumer financial services examinations with EWA product classification assessment
- TILA disclosure compliance evaluation based on current regulatory guidance interpretation
- Ability-to-repay procedure validation and consumer protection effectiveness review
- Payroll integration security and wage calculation accuracy verification
- Fee structure transparency and APR disclosure methodology examination

## Technical Implementation

The example simulates adaptive EWA compliance with regulatory classification evolution:

1. **Regulatory Classification Engine**: Dynamic compliance rule application based on evolving CFPB guidance
