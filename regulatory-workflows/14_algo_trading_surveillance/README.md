# Algorithmic Trading Surveillance (SEC Rule 17a-4/FINRA)

## Regulatory Overview

This example demonstrates comprehensive algorithmic trading surveillance compliance with SEC Rule 17a-4 business records requirements and FINRA market manipulation detection. High-frequency trading firms must maintain millisecond-precision audit trails of all order generation decisions while providing complete order book reconstruction capabilities supporting spoofing and layering investigations under Dodd-Frank Section 747 anti-manipulation provisions.

**Primary Regulations:**
- SEC Rule 17a-4 - Business Records and Retention Requirements
- FINRA Rule 4370 - Business Continuity Plans and Emergency Contact Information
- Dodd-Frank Act Section 747 - Anti-Manipulation and Anti-Disruption Provisions
- SEC Regulation SCI - Systems Compliance and Integrity Requirements
- FINRA Rule 3110 - Supervision and Surveillance of Algorithmic Trading

**Key Compliance Requirements:**
- Complete business records preservation with non-alterable and non-erasable format compliance
- Millisecond-precision order generation audit trail with algorithm version and risk parameter documentation
- Spoofing and layering pattern detection capability with surveillance alert generation and investigation support
- Order book reconstruction capability enabling complete trade timeline and market impact analysis
- Real-time surveillance integration with automated pattern recognition and escalation procedures
- Regulatory inquiry response capability with comprehensive audit trail accessibility and analysis tools
