# Robo-Advisory & SEC Regulation Best Interest (Reg BI)

## Regulatory Overview

This example demonstrates AI-powered investment recommendation compliance with SEC Regulation Best Interest (Reg BI) and FINRA suitability requirements. Investment advisors using robo-advisory platforms must ensure all automated recommendations meet the best interest standard, with comprehensive suitability analysis, conflict disclosure, and supervisory review protocols that maintain regulatory-grade audit trails.

**Primary Regulations:**
- SEC Regulation Best Interest (Reg BI) - 17 CFR 240.15l-1
- Investment Advisers Act of 1940 - 15 USC 80b-1
- FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability) - Customer-Specific Suitability
- FINRA Rule 3110 (Supervision) - Supervisory Systems and Procedures
- SEC Investment Company Act of 1940 - Mutual Fund and ETF Recommendations

**Key Compliance Requirements:**
- Best interest standard application with client-specific analysis and documentation
- Suitability determination with risk profile alignment and experience validation
- Conflict of interest identification and disclosure requirements
- Fee reasonableness analysis with cost-benefit justification
- Supervisory review procedures with escalation and approval workflows
- Fiduciary duty documentation and ongoing monitoring obligations

## Business Context

A registered investment advisor operates AI-powered robo-advisory services providing automated portfolio construction, rebalancing, and investment recommendations across retail and high-net-worth client segments. The platform must ensure every algorithmic recommendation meets Reg BI best interest standards while maintaining comprehensive audit trails supporting SEC examination requirements and FINRA supervisory obligations.

**Common Examination Focus Areas:**
- SEC investment advisor compliance examinations with Reg BI implementation assessment
- FINRA suitability rule compliance reviews with supervisory procedure validation
- Best interest standard application across diverse client profiles and risk tolerances
- Conflict of interest management and disclosure effectiveness evaluation
- Supervisory review adequacy and escalation procedure compliance verification

## Technical Implementation

The example simulates comprehensive robo-advisory compliance with AI-powered recommendation validation:

1. **Client Risk Profiling**: Comprehensive risk tolerance assessment with regulatory suitability factors
2. **AI Recommendation Engine**: Machine learning-powered portfolio construction with best interest analysis
3. **Suitability Validation**: Automated compliance checking against client profile and experience
4. **Best Interest Scoring**: Quantitative analysis of recommendation alignment with client needs
5. **Supervisory Review Workflow**: Structured approval process with escalation triggers and documentation
6. **Conflict Management**: Systematic identification and disclosure of compensation arrangements
