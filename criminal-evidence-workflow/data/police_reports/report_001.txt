INCIDENT REPORT
Case Number: 2024-00147
Date/Time: 2024-03-15 02:47
Location: 1234 Elm Street, Oakland, CA 94602
Reporting Officer: Officer R. Martinez, Badge #4471
Incident Type: Residential Burglary (PC 459)

NARRATIVE:
On March 15, 2024, at approximately 0247 hours, I, Officer R. Martinez, was dispatched to 1234 Elm Street, Oakland, CA in response to an activated residential alarm. Upon arrival at 0254 hours, I observed that the rear-facing kitchen window on the east side of the residence had been forced open. The window frame showed pry marks consistent with a flat-head tool, and glass fragments were scattered across the kitchen counter and floor.

The homeowner, Mr. David Park (DOB: 06/12/1978), arrived at the scene at approximately 0315 hours after being contacted by the alarm company. Mr. Park confirmed that he had left the residence at approximately 1830 hours the previous evening to visit family and had not returned. He identified the following items as missing: one Apple MacBook Pro laptop (silver, 16-inch, serial number C02G71ABMD6T, approximate value $2,400), one gold necklace with diamond pendant (approximate value $1,800), and approximately $350 in cash from a bedroom dresser drawer.

A neighbor, Jane Chen, residing at 1230 Elm Street, reported hearing a loud sound at approximately 0230 hours. Ms. Chen looked out her second-floor window and observed an unknown male fleeing the Park residence heading eastbound on foot toward MLK Jr. Way. She described the individual as approximately 5 feet 10 inches tall, wearing a dark-colored hoodie and blue jeans. She was unable to identify the individual's face due to the hood and distance.

I secured the scene and requested crime scene technicians. CST Unit 7 (Tech. L. Washington) arrived at 0340 hours and processed the scene. Latent fingerprints were lifted from the exterior and interior surfaces of the kitchen window frame. Additionally, security camera footage from a Ring doorbell camera at 1238 Elm Street (belonging to neighbor Mr. Tom Reeves) captured a figure matching the suspect description walking westbound on Elm Street at 0225 hours and then running eastbound at approximately 0235 hours.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
- Latent fingerprints from kitchen window frame (interior and exterior), submitted to DOJ for AFIS comparison
- Ring doorbell camera footage from 1238 Elm Street (0200-0300 hours), copied to evidence USB drive
- Photographs of forced entry point, interior disturbance, and surrounding area (32 images)
- Glass fragments collected from kitchen counter for toolmark analysis
