Incident Type: Aggravated Assault (PC 245(a)(1))

NARRATIVE:
On April 10, 2024, at approximately 2135 hours, I, Officer J. Nakamura, responded to a report of an assault in progress at the 500 block of Lake Merritt Boulevard near the amphitheater. Upon arrival at 2142 hours, I found the victim, Mr. Andre Williams (DOB: 02/28/1990), seated on a park bench with visible injuries to his face and left arm. Oakland Fire Department paramedics were already on scene providing first aid. Mr. Williams had a laceration above his left eye requiring stitches, bruising on his left cheekbone, and abrasions on his left forearm consistent with defensive wounds.

Mr. Williams stated that he was walking along the lakeside path when he was approached by two individuals who demanded his wallet and phone. When he refused, one of the individuals struck him multiple times. Mr. Williams was able to break free and call 911 from a nearby bystander's phone.

Three witnesses were present but provided substantially conflicting accounts:

Witness 1 — Lisa Tran (jogger on lakeside path): Ms. Tran stated she saw three individuals (not two) surround Mr. Williams near the amphitheater steps. She described the primary aggressor as a tall Black male wearing a red jacket and dark pants. She stated the assault lasted "about two minutes" and that one of the individuals took an object from the victim's pocket before fleeing southbound along the lake path. She reported hearing the aggressor say "give it up" before the first strike.

Witness 2 — Michael O'Brien (seated on nearby bench): Mr. O'Brien stated he observed two individuals approach Mr. Williams. He described the primary aggressor as a Hispanic male of average height wearing a dark blue or black jacket. Mr. O'Brien stated the altercation was "very quick, maybe 30 seconds" and that the individuals fled westbound across Bellevue Avenue toward the Grand Lake neighborhood. He did not observe any items being taken. He stated the encounter appeared to begin as a verbal argument that escalated.

Witness 3 — Priya Sharma (walking her dog on adjacent path): Ms. Sharma stated she saw the end of the altercation and observed two individuals running away from Mr. Williams. She described them as "two young men, one in a hoodie and one in a light-colored jacket" but could not determine race or height due to limited lighting. She stated they fled eastbound toward the 580 freeway overpass. Ms. Sharma also stated that she heard Mr. Williams shout "I know you" at his attackers as they ran, which contradicts Mr. Williams' statement that the assailants were unknown to him.

The victim was transported to Highland Hospital for treatment. No suspects were located during an area search.
