Incident Type: Grand Theft — Retail (PC 487)

NARRATIVE:
On March 22, 2024, at approximately 1645 hours, I, Officer D. Reyes, responded to a report of a retail theft at Bay Area Electronics, located at 3200 Lakeshore Avenue, Oakland, CA. The store manager, Mr. Henry Yamamoto, reported that a significant shoplifting incident had just occurred.

According to Mr. Yamamoto, at approximately 1630 hours, two individuals entered the store. The first individual, described as a white female approximately 25-30 years old with light brown or blonde hair wearing a dark green jacket, approached employee Maria Santos near the front register and asked multiple questions about product warranties and return policies. This interaction appeared designed to distract store staff.

While Ms. Santos was engaged, store security cameras captured the second individual, described as a white male with short hair wearing a gray or dark-colored top, accessing the premium smartphone display case at the rear of the store. The display case was opened without visible force — Mr. Yamamoto believes a magnetic security key was used, though the store's keys were all accounted for after the incident.

The male suspect removed four Apple iPhone 15 Pro units (retail value $999 each) from the display. The items were placed into a large shopping bag. Both individuals then exited through the store's rear emergency door onto Rand Avenue at approximately 1638 hours. The door alarm sounded but the suspects were not apprehended. A customer in the parking lot, Mr. George Patterson, observed the two individuals enter a light-colored SUV and depart northbound on Rand Avenue.

Total estimated loss: $3,996 (four iPhones at $999 each).

Mr. Yamamoto expressed concern about the sophistication of the theft and noted that the store has experienced an increase in organized retail theft over the past six months. He requested increased patrol presence in the area.
